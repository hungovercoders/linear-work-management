# Teams

Teams own issues and do the work. Multiple teams can belong to a project; initiatives
span multiple projects.

## Principles

- **Teams own issues.** Projects can involve several teams.
- **Additive only.** A team may *add* local states or labels to fit its workflow, but
  must not diverge from the canonical set — accurate cross-team insight depends on shared
  values. See [States](states.md) and [Labels](labels.md).
- **Sensible defaults.** Recommended team settings keep estimation, cycles and triage
  consistent.
