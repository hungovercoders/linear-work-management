# Projects

Projects describe **what** is going to be done, **how**, and **how it furthers an
initiative**. They are where delivery is planned and made visible.

## The standard (summary)

- **Links to an initiative.** Standalone projects are an exception and get questioned —
  work should align to strategy.
- **Single lead.** One accountable lead (typically an engineer or product owner), plus an
  owner, milestones and dates.
- **Capex / opex identified** at this level.
- **Lifecycle states** from idea → delivery → closed, reflecting the full product lifecycle.
- **Dependencies drawn** between projects — they clarify milestones and priorities.
- **Minimal, useful labels** — a taxonomy that helps without admin burden.
